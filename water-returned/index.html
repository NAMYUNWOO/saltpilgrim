Salt Pilgrim: The Water Returned

Nations rise, fall. You roam.

A 1-bit text RPG where a whole world runs on its own, on a screen modeled on an old DOS file manager.

THE GAME

  • Nations and factions rise, split off and collapse. Settlements are founded, grow and fall to ruin, and you can raise them again.
  • Caravans and raids move prices. Every morning the newspaper reports wars, truces and shortages, and every old paper is kept.
  • Talk, trade, steal. People remember what you did.
  • Places are folders, people and things are files. Enter a folder to travel; delete a monster's file to fight it. Cast rm -f to shred, fsck to repair.
  • You don't craft, you compile: a Turbo C-style build window turns raw materials into gear.
  • Die and you get a core dump, then the screen rewinds you to your feet. Sail past the horizon; no boat has ever come back.

iPhone · Android · Windows · macOS — English and Korean — plays offline, no ads, no in-app purchases.
