Salt Pilgrim

Play now, or hold one more turn for a bigger hand?

A 1-bit deckbuilding roguelike. Slay the Spire's turns meet Balatro's poker hands, on a pilgrimage across Tuzkum, a salt desert where the old world's machines lie in ruins.

THE GAME

  • Pick up to five cards and play them as one hand: pairs, straights, flushes. Damage is chips × mult.
  • Cards you don't play stay in your hand. Take a hit this turn to build a bigger hand for the next. Two discards per fight.
  • Descend twenty floors of ruins, caravan roads and nests. A 2×2 boss guards every fifth floor.
  • Start from one of seven callings, each with its own deck.
  • Or wander: walk the land map from your yurt, search the places you find, farm and build.
  • Every frame is drawn in 12×12 one-bit tiles.

Out now on the App Store. Android and Steam coming soon. English, Korean, Japanese and Chinese.

FOR FANS OF

If you like Caves of Qud (CoQ), the world is built in its spirit: the ruins of a high-tech past, factions with old grudges, and water as both money and rite. If Balatro's poker hands and the turn-by-turn tension of Slay the Spire are your thing, that is the combat.
